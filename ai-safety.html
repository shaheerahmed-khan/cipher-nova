AI AWARENESS

Responsible Generative AI

Learn how to use AI tools critically, safely, and responsibly.

Key Topics

Six things to understand before you trust or share AI output.

Basics

What is generative AI?

Generative AI creates text, images, or audio by learning patterns from large amounts of data. It predicts plausible output and does not check facts.

Accuracy

AI hallucinations

AI can state false information confidently, such as invented sources, fake quotes, or wrong statistics. Always check important claims against a trusted source.

Synthetic Media

Deepfakes and synthetic media

Deepfakes are AI-made images, audio, or video that imitate real people. Check the source, look for visual or audio glitches, and use reverse image search.

Related scams: Phishing Guide
Privacy

Privacy risks

Prompts may be stored or reused. Never paste passwords, ID or bank details, private messages, or confidential documents into AI tools.

Misinformation

Bias and misinformation

AI can repeat stereotypes and spread false stories. Compare answers with several independent sources before sharing.

Learning

Academic integrity

Follow your course's AI policy. Use AI to learn, not to replace your own work, and disclose its use when required.
